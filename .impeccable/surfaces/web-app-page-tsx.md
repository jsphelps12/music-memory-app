# Landing page (web/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: people who find Soundtracks organically, music people who want to keep what songs mean to them. Action: download on the App Store. Secondary: a quiet door to albums you make together (occasions, being rebuilt). Proof: real app screens, a faithful Lock Screen widget, demo journal entries shown as entries (never testimonials), a founder note from Josh (draft, needs his approval). No invented counts, quotes or press.

Chosen structure: "One song's life" (seed 68bb7993, candidate one-song). Code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page follows one song through the product: heard on a night drive, caught from the Lock Screen, written about, and returned to seven years later. It refuses the category page: centered hero, feature cards with icons, testimonial carousel, pricing table.

OWN-WORLD: The app's world. Paper #FBF7F2 and ink #23222B, terracotta #D9693F used for one thing at a time, night #0E0B0A for the drive. DM Serif Display titles, Newsreader for anything a person wrote, DM Sans for UI. Album artwork is the only saturated color. Hairline dividers, no boxes.

STORY: You recognize the moment (a song on the drive), see how fast it is caught, see that writing can be one line or pages, then feel the payoff when it comes back years later. Then: albums with other people, a note from the person who made it, download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed night-drive photograph. Left, the headline "Some songs hold a whole summer." with one line and the App Store badge. Right, an iPhone at true scale showing the Lock Screen with the Soundtracks widget: "Capture a song."

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: As you scroll, the same song travels: the phone's screen advances from Lock Screen to Capture to the written entry, and the date counter in the margin runs forward from 2019 to "seven years later".

RISK: Scroll storytelling must read fully without motion and on a phone; every chapter stands alone as text plus image.
