# Photo sources

Both are free-license Unsplash photographs (Unsplash License: free for
commercial use, no attribution required), re-encoded with cwebp at 2400 and
1200 px wide.

- `drive-*.webp` (hero): "person driving car during night time",
  https://unsplash.com/photos/person-driving-car-during-night-time--UAmLjpbFis
- `driver-*.webp` (Lock Screen wallpaper, founder note): "a blurry photo of a
  person in a car at night",
  https://unsplash.com/photos/a-blurry-photo-of-a-person-in-a-car-at-night-R_Umtq1j6gA

App screens in `../screens/` are simulator captures of the Soundtracks beta
showing a demo journal (staging account), not real users' entries.
