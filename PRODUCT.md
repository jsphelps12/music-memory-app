# Product

## Platform

ios

The product is an iOS app (Expo / React Native). Its web surfaces live in `web/` (Next.js): the marketing landing page, privacy and terms, and the no-app share pages (`/m/` a shared moment, `/c/` an album and its guest contribution flow, `/friend/` an invite).

## Users

Primary: the owner, Josh, and people like him who live with music and want to keep what songs mean to them. Three situations, all first-class:

- a song that brought something back (a memory);
- new music they're exploring (present tense, most days);
- a journal entry with a song attached (the song anchors; the writing is the point).

Secondary (the second front door, being rebuilt): people making something together for an occasion or a person: weddings, group birthday gifts, anniversaries, memorials. Guests contribute from a link or QR code without installing anything.

## Product Purpose

A personal music journal. Capture a song (Apple Music fills in what's playing; search; or identify what's playing nearby) and write a line, pages, or nothing. Years later the journal brings it back: On this day, weekly rewinds, browse by mood, people, year and album. North star: Josh opens it most days and it feels good to.

Business shape (decided 2026-10): one engine, two front doors. The journal is free and found organically; occasions and keepsakes (playlist, a keepsake page that plays, printed books, cards and prints carrying Soundtracks codes) are the paid layer, fully automated.

## Positioning

The song is the index of a life. Other journals organize by date; photo apps by place; music apps by plays. Soundtracks keeps the song, the moment and your own words together, and uses the song to bring the memory back.

## Operating Context

- Capture happens in the moment: from the floating + button, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center and the Action button.
- Apple Music is the primary service (now playing, search, previews, full playback for subscribers). Spotify is search/links only at current scale.
- Shared moments and albums are opened by people who may never install anything; the web page must deliver the whole memory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Journal, Rediscover (On this day, weekly rewind, browse by mood/people/year/album, search), You (shared with you, albums, friends, settings); list, calendar and map views; full-page writer; drafts.
- Widgets and iOS 18 controls; Identify via ShazamKit; 30-second previews everywhere, full playback via the user's own service.
- Licensing: copyrighted songs are never rendered into exported audio or video; playback goes through the listener's service.
- Undecided: occasion pricing, print partner, subscription.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Soundtracks. The beta is "Soundtracks β".
- The app's committed visual system (2026-10 redesign): paper and ink, one terracotta accent used sparingly, DM Serif Display for titles, Newsreader for writing, DM Sans for UI, moods as words, artwork as the color. The launch screen is a small serif wordmark on paper.
- Voice: plain, warm, specific; never salesy. AI may ask, organize, resurface and narrate; it never writes a person's reflection.

## Product Principles

1. An entry is a song plus whatever you feel like writing, from nothing to pages.
2. Fewer, better surfaces; cutting what doesn't earn its place is progress.
3. Pull, don't nag: bring people something worth reacting to; never notify on absence.
4. The no-app recipient gets the whole memory; an install ask is never a gate.
5. Never paywall capture or your own history.
