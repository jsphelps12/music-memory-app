# What's New — 1.4.0 (draft, 2026-11-17)

App Store limit: 4,000 characters. Plain, no hype, nothing invented.

---

Your moments, played back. Open a moment's photos and they play as a film, with its song underneath. Add videos too: they play straight from your Photos library, and when you turn a clip's sound on, the song dips under it.

Last week. Every Sunday evening, the songs you added and played and the photos you took that week, ready to become moments. Pick a song and that day's photos come with it.

From before. Old songs from your library, paired with the photos from the week you added them. It's at the end of Rediscover.

Hold the + button for more ways to start: identify what's playing, search, Last week or From before.

Smarter picks, on your phone. Soundtracks looks at your photos on the device to skip screenshots and receipts and find the ones worth keeping. Nothing leaves your phone.

Notifications your way. Choose how often memories come back, pick the days for your check-in, and friends' shares arrive quietly at night.

Also new: lock the app with Face ID, export your whole journal as a PDF, hold a moment for quick edit and delete, and a lot of polish everywhere.
